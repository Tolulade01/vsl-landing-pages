## Strategy Behind the Pages

These are campaign landing pages rather than general website homepages.

Each page is designed around:

1. A specific target audience
2. A clear customer problem
3. A strong value proposition
4. A focused offer
5. Lead qualification
6. A clear call to action
7. Sales follow-up

A key differentiator intentionally highlighted throughout the landing pages is:

**24/7 Real Customer Support**

The idea is to make customer support a visible buying reason rather than simply another technical feature.

## Lead Generation Funnel

**Ad → Landing Page → Lead Capture → Qualification → Sales Follow-up → Customer → Upsell / Renewal**

For a live campaign, the forms could be connected to:

- CRM
- Google Sheets
- WhatsApp
- Email notifications
- Lead-routing workflows
- Sales pipeline

The forms in this demo are intentionally non-functional.
